How it works

This isn't step one. It's step three.

Chasing sponsors before anyone's watching is a waste of everyone's time. Brands pay for audiences, not potential. That's why our Commercial service isn't something you buy on day one. It's what we build after your name is big enough that the money is real.

Get known first — that's our Promotion and Production services. Once the audience is there, our Commercial service turns it into income. Try to skip the line and you're selling something nobody's buying yet.

When it opens, it usually opens on a moment: the week after a win, a clip that runs away from you, sponsors landing in your DMs, a main-card or title-shot booking. That's when your name is worth the most it has ever been worth, and that's when we move on it.

Paid Appearances & Seminars

Deal-Flow Management

One commercial operation handling the money side, so it doesn't become a second job the week of a fight.

Brokerage only. We bring you commercial revenue: sponsors, appearances, seminars, product, licensing. We do not negotiate your purse, we do not touch your fight contracts, and we do not manage you. We work with your manager, not around them.

The structure

We only win when you win.

Our Commercial service runs on commission, not a retainer. We don't get paid unless you get paid, which means we only push the deals that are actually worth your name, and we're on the hook to make them real.

No access fee. No monthly fee. No paying for potential. Nothing costs you anything until it has made you something. You earn, then we share in it. If you close something yourself, you keep all of it.

Commission applies only to revenue we bring in. Your purse is yours: your manager and your gym already have their share of it, and we are never a claimant on top.

The rest is a conversation, not a price list. Commercial isn't sold on its own, and the numbers are set with you once we know what's actually on your table.
