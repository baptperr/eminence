Who this is for

Some camps deserve a crew on the ground.

A crew on the ground for fight week, shoot days through camp, and a full production line pointed at one fighter. That's what turns a camp into something people follow while it's happening, rather than something they hear about after the result. It's built for the camps where the moment is big enough to carry it.

The difference isn't how big your name is. Our Promotion service builds a name at any level, and it builds it well. The Production service is about crew and logistics: it's for the camps where there's genuinely something to film, something riding on the result, and no good reason for you to be the one holding the phone.

Our Production service make sense when:

The fight carries it: a main card, a title shot, or a name opposite you that people already argue about
The camp is worth filming: a training block, a life around it, and a personality that holds attention once a camera is pointed at it
There's room for a crew: you're willing to have us on the ground, and available for the parts that genuinely need you
The money makes sense: the purse, or a deal behind it, absorbs the production without you feeling it for a year

If none of that is true for this camp, we'll tell you, and we'll point you at our Promotion service instead.

The structure

Quoted per fight. Capacity is limited.

Our Production service isn't a bigger menu than our Promotion service; it's a staffed operation pointed at one fighter. A crew, a production line, and the founder running them.

Every camp is quoted. Where yours lands depends on what it takes: where you are, how many days we're on the ground, how much gets produced, and the size of the fight — a title shot with beef isn't a prelim. Travel sits inside the quote and is never billed on top. We scope it with you before either of us commits to anything.

Half at camp start, half after your purse is paid, by transfer rather than card. We never take a cut of your purse. We invoice after it reaches you.

We ask for no commitment. One camp at a time, decided when there's a camp to decide on. That's the whole fee. Sponsorships and other commercial deals run separately through our Commercial service, on commission. We never touch your fight contracts and never conflict with your manager.

Our risk reversal for established athletes is built around accountability and results, not beginner milestones. We're finalizing the exact terms on our first athletes so the promise reflects what we've actually delivered.
