How FIRST LIGHT Promotion works.

I

We define your brand.

In the first week, we work closely to find your story, your market position, your angle. We're not inventing a persona or handing you lines; we're sharpening and emphasizing what's already there. Everything else is built on it.

II

Camp opens and the work goes dense.

From the announcement to the walk, you get direction daily, a call every week, and a fight-week pack that tells you what to say and when. You film it on your phone, in your words, and post. It's minutes around your training, not hours, and you never open the app wondering what to put out.

III

The fight ends and we keep you warm.

Between fights the pace drops and so does the bill. Batched ideas, a call every two weeks, a scorecard every month. Enough that your name doesn't go quiet in the months nobody has booked you, not enough to become another thing you have to train for.

IV

Your name starts working.

Camp after camp, the audience grows, the recognition compounds, and your name turns into something brands and platforms come looking for. You focus on your sport; the name builds alongside it, until it's worth cashing in.
